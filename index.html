Use math to cast magic spells

Every time you solve a math problem, your hero casts magic, uses a shield, or attacks a monster.

Questions that fit your level

The game gives you easier or harder math questions based on how you answer, so you can learn without feeling stuck.

Practice anytime with no pressure

Visit the Training Dummy in town to practice addition and subtraction for as long as you want with zero game-over risk.

Explore and play with friends

Travel through forests, deserts, and icy mountains. Share a room code to solve math challenges together with your classmates.

Installing adds Chronicles of Arithmos directly to your device home screen so it launches straight into the game!

  1. Tap the Share button (bottom toolbar).
  2. Scroll down & tap 'Add to Home Screen'.

  1. Tap the menu button (⋮ top-right).
  2. Tap 'Add to Home screen' or 'Install app'.

Privacy Policy

Chronicles of Arithmos is designed as a safe, privacy-first educational math game for primary school students.

1. Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or sell any personal student data, email addresses, or identifying information.

2. Local Browser Storage: Game progress, badges earned, and volume settings are stored strictly on your local web browser.

3. Child Safety Guarantee: The game contains zero third-party ads, zero tracking scripts
