    Chronicles of Arithmos operates serverless without requiring user registration, email addresses, or online accounts.

    Because save data remains entirely on your device and is not stored on a cloud database, clearing your browser history, cache, or site data will reset your save file. Automatic cross-platform cloud synchronization between desktop and web builds is not supported, and deleted local saves cannot be restored by the developers.

  6. Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer Usage

    Cooperative multiplayer operates through a serverless peer-to-peer (P2P) room code architecture without centralized game servers or accounts.

    Multiplayer sessions are host-dependent. If the host player closes the game or disconnects from the network, the session terminates for all connected peers. Connection stability, latency, and timer synchronization depend on the host and participating peers' local network conditions.
