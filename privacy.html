Student Privacy & Safety

Privacy Policy

Chronicles of Arithmos is built from the ground up to provide a safe, transparent, and completely private educational math experience for young learners.

Effective Date: Academic Year 2025–2026 • Target Audience: Primary School Students (Grades 1–6) & Educators

Our Core Privacy Pledge

We believe that educational software should never compromise child privacy. We do not require accounts, we do not track student behavior across the internet, and we do not collect, monetize, or transmit any personal data.

1

Zero Personal Data Collection

When you or your students use or play Chronicles of Arithmos:

  • No Account Registration: Students do not create an account, log in, or provide any email address or username.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We never ask for, collect, or store student real names, ages, phone numbers, location data, or school IDs.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not collect hardware identifiers, IP addresses for profiling, or browser fingerprinting metrics.
2

Local Storage & Gameplay Saves

To allow players to resume their adventure, earned badges, math skill levels, inventory items, and sound volume preferences are saved exclusively using standard client-side localStorage within the user's web browser.

Important: All save data stays 100% on the local computer or tablet. It is never transmitted across the network to our academic servers or any third parties.

3

Child Safety & Ad-Free Guarantee

Primary school students deserve a distraction-free learning environment focused entirely on mathematical confidence and problem solving.

🚫 Zero Third-Party Ads No banner ads, popups, video promotions, or sponsored commercial links.
🔒 Zero Tracking Scripts No cross-site tracking, behavioral analytics, or third-party marketing pixels.
💎 Zero In-App Purchases No paywalls, loot mechanics, or commercial transactions. Fully free for education.
🛡️ Child-Centric Security Compliant with the core principles of COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection).
4

Managing & Clearing Your Data

Because all save files and game preferences reside entirely in your browser's local storage:

  • You can delete or reset all game progress at any time by clearing your browser's site data / cache.
  • Using the in-game options menu allows you to overwrite, start a new adventure, or clear saved slots.
  • Uninstalling the Progressive Web App (PWA) or removing the PC game folder will automatically erase locally stored data from your machine.
5

Academic Context & Questions

Chronicles of Arithmos is an undergraduate academic capstone project focused on gamified learning methodologies and educational technology research.

If you are an educator, parent, or researcher with inquiries regarding this privacy policy or the game's mechanics, feel free to reach out to the project research team through the academic repository.
